01The welcome, and what’s new

Once per person, on their first canvas: a dialog in the middle over the wizard shade, four pages, a demo on top (16:10), 01 / 04, [skip] on every page, [‹ back] [next ›], ← → ↵. What's new is the same frame once per release: a page per change with [show me], the smaller ones on a + N more list page, [close] instead of [skip]. Shown: welcome pages 1 and 3 (editor), what's new pages 1 and 4.

aThe wizard windowwelcome:arecommended

The same frame as the matrix, table and deck wizards (tabs 01 CANVAS 02 PIECES… with the magenta underline, the finder's corner marks), so the first window anyone sees is the one they meet again a minute later. The demo gets the most room, the words sit on solid black (best to read), and the four tabs show how long it is at a glance. What's new reuses it as is: the tabs become the changes, the last one + 2 MORE.

What’s new in this look
bInk on the pagewelcome:b

No window: the demo is a piece (graphite edge, band, its name cut in), the words are ink under it like an H1 with its small line (01 ── WELCOME · 04) and fading rule, each line on the proposed --ink-shade with a feathered edge, over the normal 72 % wizard shade. It looks like the canvas itself talking; with no box the dialog's edge is less clear, and the busy canvas still shows between the lines.

What’s new in this look
cThe transcriptwelcome:c

A terminal log: $ north-star --welcome, each page a line (✓ done, › now, · next), the current one typed out with a magenta block cursor; the demo on the right. Bold, and the log doubles as a table of contents you can click. Costs: the demo is smaller (432 wide vs 620).

02The lesson card

Beside its anchor, never over it, a short magenta lead to it; demo on top (4:3), title, body, 1 / 3, [next ›] / [got it], ×, a quiet don't show tips; closes itself with ✓ done when the person does what it teaches. Placement: right of the anchor, else left, else above; if the space there holds cards, it first shifts sideways into a clear area (in focus: left before right, keeping the right free, D-211) and covers cards only when nothing is clear; else the bottom-middle card. In focus it sits straight above the dock when that's clear. Demos are 16:10 everywhere, as the scenes are authored. Each option: by a console, by the dock, with no demo, with no room beside its anchor, and the done → next handover.

aThe prompt cardlesson:arecommended

A small box in the prompt's look: ┌ LESSON · CONSOLE, the right panel's magenta corner marks, a straight lead with a square tip on the anchor. It's a box on solid black, so it reads on any canvas (busy planes, light theme), and it goes anywhere (console, dock, toast, panel, side nav, corner) the same way. 268 px wide; without a demo it's just shorter. Its actions ([‹ guide] when played from ?) stay lowercase.

✓ done → the next lesson (1.2 s done, 320 ms break, 300 ms of nothing, then it builds)

Ink, no box: a magenta hairline down its left like a paragraph's, the lead joining it; the name a small line (2/2 ── CONSOLE · LESSON); every text block on the proposed --ink-shade with a feathered edge, all shades one layer under all the text (so a shade never paints over the line before it), the demo with a halo of it. Shown over a busy table: it now reads, but the gaps between lines still show the piece, and without a box it's less clear where to click.

✓ done → the next lesson
cThe drawerlesson:c

The anchor opens: the lesson hangs out of the piece's bottom edge under the console (or rises out of the dock's or toast's top edge), joined with no gap, the demo beside the words. Without a demo it's one column, at least 320 px, centred on its anchor; where it's wider than the anchor, its open edge is magenta out to the anchor's corners. With no edge to hang from (the fallback, or played from ?) it rises from the screen's bottom edge or becomes a plain box. Its "done" can't fold back into the console after a focus (the console is gone), so it breaks in place like the others.

Each option: on the canvas, in a plane's focus, after typing pl, and after [hide guide]. Words from onboarding-lessons.md §2.

One panel, split by a hairline: the menu left, the guide right, the same height, the two feet one line. Typing can't shrink it below the guide's own height (its floor, measured here: 338 px with a 16:10 demo), so the guide always fits. [hide guide] hides it for good; one line says ? brings it back.

Shown over the canvas's table and deck and over cards in a focus: it reads, and needs no floor when typing narrows the menu. But it moves on every arrow press, and the canvas still shows around it.

Every area at once, in the deck's ledger look: a column head 02 CANVAS ──── 6/9 with a bar of cells (seen grey, new magenta), the keys as a keycap sheet. A real "how much do I know" picture, bold. 05Nudge · empty canvas

One look each. The nudge: one line at most once a session, by the tool it's about, a short magenta lead, ink, · ×. The empty canvas: the words at the +'s height, a dashed arrow straight to it; gone at the first piece.

A token to add, and what the mocks showed

Proposed token: --ink-shade (O5 adds it to tokens.css)

  • What unframed onboarding ink sits on when it floats over pieces: each line's shade and its feathered edge (background + box-shadow: 0 0 18px 12px), and a halo round an ink option's demo.
  • Dark rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9) (the page black at 90 %); light rgba(245, 245, 245, 0.92) (the page paper #f5f5f5 at 92 %). The app's own unframed ink uses --bg at 80 % / 72 %, enough on the empty canvas, not over a busy piece. Only the ink options (welcome:b, lesson:b, kind:b) need it.

What the mocks showed about the spec

  • Demo shapes: scenes are authored at 16:10 (§6.2), so every demo is 16:10 now (lesson cards and the kind guide too, no 4:3).
  • "Same height as the menu" breaks when typing narrows it. Typed pl, the + menu is one row. The glued column needs a floor: the guide's own height (measured: 338 px for kind:a, 400 for the man page), drawn in kind a and c; the ink option needs none.
  • The canvas + menu is tall (≈ 400 px; in a plane ≈ 450). From the side nav's + it runs off a 768 px laptop; floating.ts must lift it, and the guide with it.
  • A lesson card is ≈ 340 px tall with a 16:10 demo; beside a console near the bottom it fits only left or right. Placement: right, left, above; over cards, shift sideways into a clear area first; then the bottom-middle fallback (drawn for each option). In focus: above the dock, the right kept free.
  • The ? guide is taller than a laptop (≈ 790 px with one area open): at most the screen minus 96 px, the list scrolls.
  • # keys is ≈ 38 rows and every area 3–11: all fold ([▸ N inside]), one open at a time.
  • "✓ done for a moment" needs numbers: 1.2 s, then the 320 ms break, 300 ms of nothing, then the next one builds. The next lesson waits for that, so two never overlap. A done after a focus shows where the card was; the anchor (the console) is gone.
  • lesson:c only hangs from edges (console, dock, toast); everywhere else it falls back to a plain box, so it's two looks in one.
  • The empty-canvas hint reads best at the +'s height (a little above the middle) with a straight arrow; the old "or drop a .md file here" line is gone (it isn't in the lessons' words).
  • guide:c can't fit 61 notes at once at 1500 × 640; it ends every column with + N more.
